🧪How It Works

  1. Create Your Diagram: Design your flowchart in FlowDigm as usual
  2. Export as DOCX: Click Export → DOCX format
  3. Open in Word: The document contains your diagram image plus editable sections
  4. Click to Edit: Click any gray placeholder text to replace with your content
  5. Format & Enhance: Use Word's tools to format, add comments, track changes
  6. Share & Collaborate: Perfect for team collaboration and documentation
